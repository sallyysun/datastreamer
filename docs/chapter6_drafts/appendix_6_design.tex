\section{Chapter 6 implementation details}\label{app:ch6-design}

\subsection{Exponentially weighted marginal}
Each variable's empirical CDF is $F_t(x) = \sum_{i\le t} a_{t,i}\mathbb 1\{X_i \le x\}$ with $a_{t,i} \propto \kappa^{t-i}$ and $\kappa = (N_{\mathrm{eff},m}-1)/(N_{\mathrm{eff},m}+1)$, $N_{\mathrm{eff},m} = 500$.
A weighted Greenwald--Khanna summary holds it in bounded memory.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Forward decay:} observation $i$ enters with weight $\kappa^{-i}$, so old tuples never need rescaling.
  \item \emph{Compression:} neighbouring tuples merge every $\lfloor 1/(2\epsilon)\rfloor$ insertions while their summed weight stays within $2\epsilon W_t$, with $\epsilon = 0.005$.
  \item \emph{Pseudo-observation:} $X_t$ maps to $\widehat U_t = (B_t + w_t)/(W_t + 2w_t)$, computed before $X_t$ is inserted. Here $B_t$ is the weight below $X_t$, read at the midpoint of its rank interval; $W_t$ is the total weight; and $w_t$ is the weight $X_t$ receives.
\end{itemize}
Without forgetting this reduces to $(R_t+1)/(t+2)$.

\subsection{Hellinger quadrature}
We write \eqref{eq:ch6-hellinger} in normal-score coordinates, $\int\!\!\int\sqrt{c_t\widehat c}\,\phi(x)\phi(y)\,dx\,dy$ with $u = \Phi(x)$ and $v = \Phi(y)$, and apply the tensor trapezoid rule on $[-8,8]^2$ with 81 nodes per axis.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Accuracy:} against the closed form for two Gaussian copulas, and against 801 nodes for tail-dependent truths and family-bank mixtures, the absolute error is below $10^{-8}$.
  \item \emph{Truncation:} the region $|x|$ or $|y| > 8$ contributes less than $2\Phi(-8) \approx 10^{-15}$, since $\sqrt{c\widehat c} \le (c+\widehat c)/2$.
  \item \emph{Evaluation times:} every 50 observations from $t = 500$, plus every 10 in the first 100 after a change. The extra times enter only the $[2500, 2600)$ window, so every window averages uniformly in time.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Why Xiao and Hermite are not scored as copula densities}
A validation fixture (seed 42, 2000 observations, bank arm settings) checks the three properties separately.
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Xiao}'s histogram is non-negative and integrates to one, but its margins deviate from uniform by up to 0.07--0.48 (mean absolute deviation 0.03--0.21).
  \item \emph{Hermite}'s series density is negative on 2--19\% of the square before clipping, integrates to 1.02--1.25 after clipping, and has non-uniform margins.
\end{itemize}
Renormalisation would fix only the total mass.
Neither is therefore a copula density, and neither enters the density comparisons.

\subsection{Memory-budget protocol (E1)}
\begin{itemize}
  \item \emph{Caps:} 16, 64 and 256\,KiB of measured retained state.
  \item \emph{Tuning:} within each cap, each estimator's approximation parameter and gain are chosen on validation seeds 1000--1039. The gains are $g\in\{0.005, 0.01, 0.02, 0.05, 0.10\}$, extended for the constant and drift paths by $\{0.0005, 0.001, 0.002\}$ and each estimator's cumulative mode (Xiao $\lambda = 0$, Hermite and recursive MPL weight $1/m$). The approximation parameters are Xiao's number of bins, Hermite's order, and the KLL sketch size $k$ of the recursive MPL's margins.
  \item \emph{Testing:} the chosen configuration is tested on seeds 42--141.
  \item \emph{Scoring:} RMSE every fifth observation from $t = 500$.
  \item \emph{Margins:} the recursive MPL here uses cumulative KLL margins (Apache DataSketches) instead of the shared exponentially weighted marginal, because E1 compares single estimators at matched memory.
\end{itemize}

\subsection{Reproducibility}
All results come from C++20 harnesses on Linux with libstdc++.
Its random-variate generators differ from libc++'s, so a seed reproduces a stream only on the same platform.
