Honours thesis · working note

Change detection for streaming copulas

Ten named detectors are one recursion with three knobs. And the exponential forgetting already in the codebase is a Kalman filter in disguise. Both are identities, not analogies — and both shorten the work.

Date25 August 2026 StatusWorking note Companionroadmap.md · todo.md

Orientation

Why this note exists

The roadmap already surveys which detectors exist and sketches BOCPD. This note does the two things that survey does not.

First, it shows that CUSUM, Shiryaev–Roberts, GLR, BOCPD and HMM regime-switching are one recursion with three knobs — not ten separate methods. That turns the thesis contribution from “we benchmarked ten detectors” (which answers to it depends) into “the design space has three axes; here is which cell wins for copulas, and why.”

Second, it develops the state-space view, which is the missing link between the exponential forgetting already implemented in src/estimators/dependence/ and the detectors we want to add. They are not two branches. One contains the other.

Part I

Two bridges

Both are identities, not analogies. Both shorten the work.

Bridge 1 · our λ is a Kalman gain

Take the local level model on zt = atanh ρt:

zt = zt−1 + ωt, ωt ~ 𝒩(0, σω2) state
yt = zt + vt, vt ~ 𝒩(0, σv2) observation

The Kalman recursion is

Pt|t−1 = Pt−1 + σω2, Ft = Pt|t−1 + σv2, Kt = Pt|t−1Ft
et = yt − ẑt−1, ẑt = ẑt−1 + Ktet

At steady state Kt → K and this collapses to:

Identity 1

ẑt = (1 − K) ẑt−1 + K yt an EWMA with λ = 1 − K
K = √q2 + 4q − q 2 , q = σω2σv2 (signal-to-noise ratio)

Consequences

  • Every fixed-λ estimator in src/estimators/dependence/ is the steady-state limit of the simplest possible SSM. The SSM is a strict generalization, not a competing branch — it slots in beside the existing estimators.
  • “Choose λ” becomes “choose q”, a signal-to-noise ratio. λ is a tuning constant; q is a quantity with a likelihood, so the data can speak to it.
  • VFF ≡ estimating q online. Same statement as the roadmap’s “the real knob is the process noise”, now with the exact map between parameterizations.
  • The transient Kt > K handles cold start automatically: the filter is wide open early and tightens as Pt shrinks. That is the principled version of the hermite_ew cold-start patch.

Bridge 2 · CUSUM is already a state-space filter

Model the change as a 2-state absorbing Markov chain — pre-change → post-change, no return. Then the same graphical model produces all four methods, differing only in the semiring and the latent alphabet:

RecursionLatent structureYields
Forward / sum-product 2-state absorbing Shiryaev–Roberts   Rt = (1 + Rt−1)Λt
Max-product (Viterbi) 2-state absorbing CUSUM   St = max(0, St−1 + log Λt)
Forward / sum-product Run-length counter BOCPD
Forward / sum-product K-state recurrent Regime-switching HMM

This makes CUSUM → SR → BOCPD → HMM one ladder rather than four unrelated citations.

Part II

The detector design space

Every method in the survey list is the same sequential recursion. Only three things vary.

KnobOptionsWho is who
Unknown change time τ max over τ  /  sum over τ CUSUM, GLR = max (Viterbi). SR, BOCPD = sum (forward filter).
Unknown post-change θ1 known plug-in / maximize / integrate against prior CUSUM = known. GLR = maximize. SR-r, BOCPD = integrate.
Input statistic raw observation / score / log-LR / kernel embedding Page–Hinkley, sequential score test, CUSUM, MMD.

Why this matters. Page–Hinkley and the EWMA chart fall out as degenerate cells — Page–Hinkley is CUSUM with a mean-shift plug-in, and the EWMA chart is the steady-state Kalman of Bridge 1 with a threshold bolted on. Neither needs a separate experiment. That is real work saved.

Regime-switching vs change-point is a hazard choice, not a paradigm

It is tempting to file HMMs under a different question — “which regime am I in?” versus “when did it change?” They are the same object. What differs is what happens to θ at a transition:

ModelLatent chainθ after a transition
CUSUM2-state, absorbingfixed known θ1
BOCPDrun-length rtdrawn fresh from the prior
HMMK-state, recurrentreused from a finite remembered set

Consequence · chapter candidate

BOCPD is amnesiac. Financial dependence regimes recur — calm → crisis → calm — and BOCPD discards everything it learned about the crisis regime the moment it leaves. An HMM re-enters a known regime instantly, with zero relearning delay. The synthesis lets a new run either reuse an old regime or spawn a new one.

Does regime memory reduce detection delay on recurring dependence shifts?

Sharp · novel · answerable · needs only the oscillate DGP we already generate

Part III

The SSM ladder

Four levels, increasing sophistication, each reusing the last.

Level 1 · SSM whitens, CUSUM detects

Run the Kalman filter of Bridge 1, then run CUSUM or Page–Hinkley on the standardized innovations et/√Ft, which are i.i.d. 𝒩(0,1) under the model.

Why this is the highest-value thing to build. It fixes distinction 5 in the roadmap — drift is the common case. CUSUM on raw ρ̂t chatters under drift because the pre-change stream is not stationary, so Lorden-type optimality does not apply. The SSM absorbs the drift, the innovations become white, and the classical theory transfers.

Division of labour

SSM handles gradual drift │ CUSUM handles what the SSM cannot absorb

Practical observation equation

Do not feed one tick at a time — the per-tick normal-score product zx,tzy,t has mean ρ and variance 1 + ρ2, and the link ρ = tanh z is nonlinear, so you would need an EKF/UKF. Instead emit a Fisher-transformed windowed Spearman every m ticks:

yt = atanh ρ̂St, σv2 ≈ 1.06m − 3

σv2 being known is a gift specific to this problem: it pins down the denominator of q, leaving a single scalar to fit. O(1) amortized, linear-Gaussian, exact filter.

Level 2 · GLR on the state

The honest version when the post-change ρ is unknown. A jump of unknown size ν at unknown time τ leaves a deterministic signature on the innovation sequence — the filter’s step response ϱs(τ) — so the GLR is a matched filter maximized over τ:

gt = maxτ ( Σs ϱs(τ) es / Fs )2 Σs ϱs(τ)2 / Fs > h

For the local level model ϱs(τ) is closed-form. This is exactly “CUSUM, but the SSM tells you what a change looks like.”

⚠ Streaming caveat

A GLR with sup over all τ ≤ t is O(t) per step and is not streaming-admissible — it breaks the central claim of the project. Use the window-limited GLR (Lai): restrict τ ∈ [t−W, t−W0], giving O(W) per step with near-optimal delay. Non-negotiable if GLR goes in.

Level 3 · switching SSMs — soft detection, no hard reset

  • Harrison & Stevens multiprocess DLM. Each step is a mixture over {normal, outlier, level shift}; collapse via GPB1 to stay O(1). Detect-and-restart without a bang-bang decision.
  • IMM filter (target-tracking literature). A bank of Kalman filters at different σω2, Markov-switched and soft-mixed. This is the roadmap’s “multi-timescale ensemble”, but with a transition model and decades of theory attached.
  • West’s cumulative Bayes factor monitoring. Ht = Bt · min(1, Ht−1) — which is literally CUSUM in log space with log-Bayes-factor increments. The tightest link between our CUSUM and the DLM world.

Level 4 · no detector at all

Give the process noise a heavy tail (Student-t) or a spike-and-slab / Laplace prior. A jump becomes just a large draw from the tail, and the filter adapts on its own. The MAP under Laplace process noise is fused-lasso / ℓ1 trend filtering; the streaming version is an online proximal step. Cheap, and it sidesteps the false-alarm-threshold question entirely.

Part IV

What the standard survey misses

Five gaps, ordered by value to this thesis.

  1. Test martingales and e-values

    The biggest gap. Every classical detector needs a threshold calibrated against a known pre-change law — but roadmap distinction 3 says rank/PIT feedback makes the pre-change stream non-i.i.d., so those ARL guarantees do not transfer. Conformal test martingales give anytime-valid false-alarm control without trusting the null. Recent literature, essentially untouched in the copula change-point space, and it solves a problem we have already identified as fundamental. Strongest novelty candidate here.

  2. The efficient (orthogonalized) score, not the raw score

    Partition θ = (θmarg, θdep). The efficient score for the dependence block — the score minus its projection onto the nuisance tangent space —

    S̃dep = Sdep − Idm Imm−1 Smarg

    — yields a CUSUM that is invariant to marginal changes by construction. This is the principled fix for distinction 2 (the marginal-vs-dependence confound), rather than hoping the ranks are clean. Fisher scoring already computes every ingredient. Score-CUSUM is also the locally most powerful test, hence optimal for small changes — exactly the copula regime. Rank this above GLR.

  3. Multivariate CUSUM on Hermite coefficients

    Every detector in the standard list is scalar, and therefore misses distinction 1 by design: a copula can change shape — tail dependence appears, asymmetry flips — at constant Spearman ρ. Take the state to be the Hermite coefficient vector at (random walk), observations hjk(Ut, Vt) — already computed in hermiter.hpp — and detect on the innovation vector:

    St = max( 0, ‖St−1 + et‖Σ−1 − k )

    No published streaming-copula equivalent that I am aware of.

  4. The optimal-delay benchmark

    Lorden / Lai:

    𝔼[delay] ≈ log(ARL0) KL(θ1 ‖ θ0)

    For Gaussian copulas the KL between ρ0 and ρ1 is closed form. So we can compute the theoretically minimal detection delay for any ρ jump and plot every detector against it. This upgrades the evaluation chapter from “we compared some things” to “here is the efficiency frontier, and how close each detector gets.”

  5. Window-limited GLR

    See the streaming caveat in Part III, Level 2. Without it, GLR is not admissible here at all.

Part V

Architecture: the loop, not the pipeline

The obvious topology is one-way. That topology is wrong — or at least uninteresting.

Identity 2

VFF and detect-and-restart are not rivals — they are one dial at different decision hardness.

The roadmap currently frames these as competing options (#1 vs #2 of “the ultimate streaming estimator”). They are the same architecture. Cleaner story, and less to build.

Part VI

Evaluation

One trap, one metric set, one question.

The trap: detectors are only comparable at matched ARL0

Every detector has a threshold h that trades false alarms against delay. Comparing two detectors at arbitrary thresholds is meaningless. Calibrate all of them to the same in-control average run length under stationarity first, then compare delay.

And because of rank feedback, ARL0 must be calibrated by simulation or bootstrap on the actual pseudo-observation stream, not read off a formula. The harness does not measure ARL0 at all yet — this is the first thing to add, before any detector number is treated as a result.

The sharpened research question

Not “how does the choice of detector affect the trade-off”, which answers to it depends. Instead:

Thesis question

How closely can a streaming, O(1), marginal-invariant copula change-point detector approach the information-theoretic detection-delay bound — and does adaptive memory dominate the entire fixed-λ family on the ARL0 / delay / stationary-variance frontier?

Sequence

Build order

A real dependency order — each step needs the one above it. Steps 1–3 alone give the thesis its cleanest single result.

  1. ARL0 calibration in the harness

    Bootstrap under stationarity. Nothing else is interpretable without it.

  2. Local-level Kalman on Fisher-z’d blockwise Spearman

    kalman_local_level.hpp, roughly 150 lines. Bridge 1 means it is a strict generalization of the fixed-λ estimators, not a new branch.

  3. Innovation CUSUM

    On et/√Ft, wired to the existing reset() hook.

  4. Efficient-score CUSUM

    Marginal-invariant by construction; reuses the Fisher-scoring machinery.

  5. Window-limited GLR

    The unknown-ρ1 comparator, kept streaming-admissible.

  6. BOCPD

    NIW-on-normal-scores, per the roadmap. The Bayesian gold standard to measure against.

  7. The novelty bets

    Test martingales · MCUSUM on Hermite coefficients · regime memory.

The headline result, if nothing else lands

The fixed-λ estimators are one point on a curve the Kalman filter parameterizes — and the drift the filter absorbs is exactly what was making CUSUM chatter.
